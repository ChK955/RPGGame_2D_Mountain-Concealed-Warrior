All PNG assets in this folder are original procedural artwork created for Mistbound.
No third-party characters, sprites, textures, or reference images are included.
Source and deterministic rebuild: scripts/generate-assets.mjs.
Actor sheets: 8 columns × 8 rows; cells 128×128; anchor (64,104).
Rows: Idle, Run, Jump, Fall, Attack, Skill, Hurt, Death.
